## Our Process

1. ### Initial Consultation
We begin with a detailed discussion of your project requirements, target specifications, and goals. This helps us understand your vision and provide accurate quotes and timelines.
2. ### Design Phase
Our engineering team develops your concept into a complete electronic design, including schematics, PCB layout, and firmware if needed. We maintain regular communication to ensure the design meets your requirements.
3. ### Design Review
We conduct a thorough review of the design, considering factors like manufacturability, cost, and performance. You receive complete documentation for approval before moving to production.
4. ### Prototype Construction
Using professional-grade equipment and following industry best practices, we carefully construct your prototype. Each step is documented and quality-checked.
5. ### Testing & Verification
Every prototype undergoes thorough testing to verify functionality and performance according to specifications. We provide detailed test results and documentation.
6. ### Small Batch Production
For clients ready to move forward, we offer small batch production runs to help transition your product toward full-scale manufacturing.
